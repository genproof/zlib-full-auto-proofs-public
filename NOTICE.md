It contains source files of zlib 1.3.2 (https://github.com/madler/zlib, commit
da607da739fa6047df13e66a2af6b8bec7c2a498, tag v1.3.2): adler32.c, compress.c, crc32.c, crc32.h, deflate.c,
deflate.h, gzguts.h, inffast.c, inffast.h, inffixed.h, inflate.c, inflate.h, inftrees.c, inftrees.h, trees.c,
trees.h, uncompr.c, zconf.h, zlib.h, zutil.c and zutil.h. Copyright (C) 1995-2026 Jean-loup Gailly and Mark Adler,
under the zlib license (LICENSE, zlib's own file). adler32.c, deflate.c, deflate.h, inffast.c, inflate.c, inflate.h,
inftrees.c, inftrees.h and zutil.c are altered: every changed line carries a `quickproof:` comment, and
patch/PATCH.md lists the changes.

The Clight files src/**/code.v are generated by CompCert's clightgen from the C files: those of the ten zlib units
are derived from zlib's files, those of the specs (src/harness/*/code.v) contain declarations from zlib's
headers, and src/libc/code.v and src/malloc/code.v come from QuickProof's libc/libc.c and libc/malloc_unit.c.

The quickproof/ submodule is QuickProof; its licence is quickproof/LICENSE.
